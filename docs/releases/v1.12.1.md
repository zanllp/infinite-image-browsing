## Faster macOS startup

The macOS desktop app no longer unpacks its backend every time you open it. The installed app uses more disk space in exchange for a shorter wait. Your library and settings stay in place.

On v1.12.0, click **Update to 1.12.1** on the welcome screen and confirm to install. On v1.11.0 or earlier, download an installer manually. For macOS 14 or later, choose the Apple Silicon (`aarch64`) or Intel (`x64`) DMG. The macOS builds remain ad-hoc signed, not notarized; see the [macOS installation notes](https://github.com/zanllp/infinite-image-browsing#macos-unsigned-preview).
